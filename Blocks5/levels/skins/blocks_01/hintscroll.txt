This file makes the hint note behave like a sheet of paper: it flies in rolled
up at both ends, unrolls as it lands, and rolls up again before it goes. Its
contents are ignored, only its existence counts, and it belongs to the skin
whose hint.png is actually loaded - a skin that reaches that picture through
default_hint.png therefore rolls as well. Leave it out where the hint graphic
is not paper: a hard-edged display panel that rolls up looks silly.
