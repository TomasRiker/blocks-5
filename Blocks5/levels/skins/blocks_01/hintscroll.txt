This file makes the hint note behave like a sheet of paper: it arrives
rolled up at both ends and unrolls once it has come to rest.

Its contents are ignored - only its presence matters, and it counts for the
skin whose hint.png is actually loaded, so a skin that borrows this one through
default_hint.png gets the rolling as well. Leave it out for a hint graphic that
is not paper: a sci-fi display panel that rolls up looks wrong.
